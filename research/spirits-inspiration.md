# Spirits — Brand & Inspiration Research

> Third in the series (after champagne + beer). Focus: **premium & luxury spirits brands** —
> the strongest overlap with ChampagneHuset's luxury-DTC and provenance storytelling.
>
> Last updated: 2026-06-25

## Why spirits is the best inspiration source

Of all the drinks categories, premium spirits has the **deepest bench of luxury brand sites**:
heritage storytelling, collector/allocation mechanics, ultra-premium product pages, and gifting
flows — all the things a grower-champagne brand needs. Grouped by sub-category below.

## What to steal from spirits (and what not to)

- **Steal:** allocation/limited-release mechanics, cask/provenance storytelling, collector tiers,
  gifting & engraving flows, and the restrained type/photography of the cognac houses.
- **Steal especially from Louis XIII** for ultra-luxury feel, and **Clase Azul** for how physical
  packaging becomes the brand's whole story — relevant to champagne limited editions.
- **Don't copy** the heavy gating/age-verification walls or slow video-first homepages — they hurt
  the fast browse/discovery experience your Systembolaget-context site needs.
