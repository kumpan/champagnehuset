## "What other categories are there?"

### Beer styles / segments worth knowing
If you mean beer *categories* (segments), the premium-relevant ones are:

- **Lager family** — Pilsner, Helles, Märzen, Bock/Doppelbock
- **Ales** — Pale Ale, IPA (incl. NEIPA / hazy), Amber, Brown
- **Belgian** — Trappist/Abbey, Tripel, Dubbel, Quad, Saison, Witbier
- **Wheat** — Hefeweizen, Witbier, Berliner Weisse
- **Stout & Porter** — Imperial Stout, Pastry Stout
- **Sour & wild** — Lambic, Gueuze, Kriek, mixed-fermentation (Brett)
- **Specialty/premium-adjacent** — Barrel-aged, low/no-alcohol (a fast-growing premium space)

### Adjacent product categories to gather inspiration batches for next
If you mean *other industries* to mine (the way we did champagne + beer), the strongest matches
for a premium-drinks brand are:

- **Wine** — Bordeaux/Burgundy estates, natural wine, premium rosé
- **Spirits** — single-malt whisky, cognac, premium gin, mezcal/tequila, rum
- **Sake & Japanese craft** (premium presentation masters)
- **Non-alc / functional drinks** (premium 0.0%, adaptogens) — booming, design-forward
- **Coffee** — specialty/third-wave roasters (best DTC + subscription UX in food/bev)
- **Adjacent luxury** — fragrance, watches, fashion, chocolate (transferable brand craft)
